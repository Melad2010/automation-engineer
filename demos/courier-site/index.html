How do I track a shipment?

Enter an air waybill (AWB) number in the tracking box. For this demo, use TVQ1001, TVQ1002, TVQ1003 or TVQ1004 to explore different shipment stages. The updates are sample records, not live delivery information.

Does Tavvique deliver to my pincode?

Try a six-digit Indian pincode in the checker. The demo includes 400001, 110001, 560001, 600001, 500001 and 380001. A match means the pincode appears in this sample dataset; it does not confirm a real delivery service.

Which service fits a heavier shipment?

Surface cargo illustrates transport for larger cartons. B2B freight illustrates consolidated business shipments. Both are sample services; the quote form can be used to explore the information a courier would need.

Can I request a pickup on this website?

You can try the quote form with fictional details. It validates your entries in the browser and shows a demo confirmation. Nothing is sent, stored or booked.

Is this a real courier company?

No. Tavvique is an invented brand for a website sample. The branch network, shipment records, service descriptions and contact details are illustrative. This site does not provide courier services.

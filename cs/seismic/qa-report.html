  • Supplementary check, calendar year 2023, all magnitudes: 14,601 rows, 13,958 earthquakes from M-0.50 to M5.08. Its peak bin is 0.9, so maximum curvature gives Mc = 1.1. b-value, maximum likelihood (Aki-Utsu, bin width 0.1): b = 0.946 ± 0.009 (Shi and Bolt 1982), from 10,870 events at or above Mc = 1.8. Mean magnitude 2.2092. a-value 5.738.

b-value, least squares on log10 of the cumulative counts, 36 bins from M1.8 to M5.3: b = 1.077 (R squared 0.993). Maximum likelihood minus least squares: -0.131. Using only bins with 10 or more events gives b = 1.033. Least squares over M1.8 to M3.2 only gives b = 0.944. Least squares gives every bin the same weight, so a few large events count as much as thousands of small ones. That is why the maximum likelihood value is the one reported.

13. Limits

  • Mixed magnitude scales. Most events use ml, the north-west corner uses md from the Northern California network, and larger events use mw. Different magnitude types need not agree, and this analysis did not compare them event by event. Section 8 gives b by subset and by magnitude range.
  • Network changes. The catalogue joins solutions from several networks. Station coverage can change over time; beyond the 2023 completeness check, this was not tested.
  • The box is a rectangle. Admin-1 units it touches: Arizona, Baja California, California, Nevada, Sonora. It also covers offshore water. Events located from outside the network (azimuthal gap above 180°) give b = 0.855, against 0.949 inside it. A lower b is what missed small events would produce. They are 3.1% of the events at or above Mc, and leaving them out moves b by +0.003. Completeness was not mapped, and one Mc is used for the whole box.
  • Aftershocks and swarms are not removed (no declustering). Right after a large event, small events are hard to detect, so counts in the first hours can be too low even above Mc. This was not corrected.
  • 111 automatic solutions may still change after review.
  • Depth is the least constrained location value, datums differ by network, and some depths are fixed.
  • Times are UTC. Magnitudes are rounded to 0.1 before binning, half up.
